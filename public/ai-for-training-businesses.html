Questions

Everything you need to know.

What does a build cost?

Most builds start from $15,000. The exact price is set from your architecture, so you know the full scope before you commit. Your $1,500 covers the session and your full architecture, and comes off the build if you continue within 30 days.

Is the session a sales call?

No. It is two hours of paid strategy on your real business, and you keep the summary whether or not you build.

What if a build is out of reach right now?

You still leave with your summary of next steps. If you want to build it yourself, I teach you one-to-one over six weeks, with AI as your execution team.

What is an AI second brain?

Everything you know, in one place your AI can search: your frameworks, session notes, client history and programme material. It means prep, follow-up and new content start from your own thinking, not a blank page.

Will AI make my work feel less human?

That's the risk I work against. AI takes on research, admin and follow-up, so you spend more time on the human work.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to know your business. The technical side is handled for you, or taught to you step by step.

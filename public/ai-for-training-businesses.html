A different way

The problem isn't your people. It's the architecture.

Most institutes grow around their decision makers, not around a system. Those are two different architectures.

How it runs today
  • Decision makers are the system
  • Programme knowledge lives in a few heads
  • Proposals and reports written from scratch
  • Tools that don't talk to each other
  • More clients means more manual work
  • Reactive, and hard to scale
vs
With an AI operating system
  • AI and process are the system
  • Knowledge documented and searchable by the whole team
  • Proposals and reports drafted from your own material
  • Tools connected into one layer
  • Growth adds capacity, not workload
  • Structured, measurable and ready to scale

Questions

Everything you need to know.

What happens on the free call?

We talk about your institute, where AI would make the biggest difference, and whether we are a fit. If we are, we agree the next step together. Either way, you keep your summary of next steps.

What do we walk away with?

A written architecture: your workflow map, where AI fits across every system and process, a recommended tool stack, and a priority sequence. It is a complete design for your operating system, clear enough to hand to anyone.

Who builds it?

Your own team or a recommended partner. You own the architecture, and I can oversee the build so it lands the way it was designed.

Can my team attend the strategy session?

Yes. Bring up to three of your leaders, including the person most responsible for operations. Having the right people in the room makes the architecture more accurate.

What tools do you work with?

The major platforms: Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini; Make, Zapier and n8n; HubSpot, GoHighLevel and Airtable; Notion and Google Workspace or SharePoint; Slack, Teams and WhatsApp Business; and the learning platforms institutes already run, such as Moodle, TalentLMS, Kajabi or Thinkific. I design around what you already use and only recommend a change when a tool is causing more friction than value.

We already use some AI tools. Is this still for us?

Yes, that gives us a foundation. Having individual AI tools is very different from having an AI operating system. The gap is usually that the tools are not connected into one system, and that is what the architecture designs.

Where do you work?

My work is virtual. I work with institutes and training businesses across Africa, the UK, Europe, North America, the Middle East and Asia. Everything runs remotely by video, and sessions are scheduled around your time zone. When an engagement needs it, you can fly me out.

Who is this not for?

It is not the right fit if you are just starting out with no established programmes or processes yet, if you want a tool handed over with no thinking behind it, or if your team is not ready to make time for the session.

Do we need to be technical?

No. You need to know how your institute runs. The architecture is written in plain language anyone on your team can act on.

How do you measure success?

The architecture sets the measures up front: hours recovered each week, proposal turnaround, enrolment time, renewal rate, and impact you can report to clients at 30 and 90 days.

The curriculum

Six tracks. Six transformations.

Not a syllabus. Not a calendar. Six areas of mastery, each one leaving you with something real you can use immediately.

Track 01
The AI Consultant Blueprint
Turn your Expert Framework into the practice you are actually building.
The framework is what you know. The blueprint is what you sell. You decide who the practice serves, which problem it solves, how an engagement is structured, and what you charge for it. Most experts spend years circling this. This track makes the decisions deliberately, so everything after it is built on ground that holds.
Track 02
Personality Intelligence for Sales
Read how a buyer decides, then sell to that.
Every buyer has a pattern: what they fear, what they trust, and what finally moves them to say yes. You learn to read that pattern in conversation, and to shape your discovery, your pitch, and your follow-up around it. This is psychology applied to selling. Not scripts, not tricks, not pressure.
Track 03
Client Discovery, Operating System Builds and Business Architecture
Diagnose a client's business, architect the solution, then build it.
How to run discovery calls, diagnose a client's business, present what you find, and close the engagement. Then how to architect the whole practice around it: your offer, your delivery, your pricing, and the path a buyer takes from first contact to signed scope. Finally, how to design, build, and hand over an AI operating system for a paying client: the infrastructure, the automation, the implementation. You stop giving advice and start delivering things clients depend on.
Track 04
The Automated Leads Engine
A system that finds and warms buyers while you work.
A leads engine that works while you are not. You set up who you target, how the first message reaches them, and how follow-up happens without you chasing. Not volume for its own sake. A steady flow of the right conversations, so getting clients stops depending on how many hours you spent networking.
Track 05
AI Agents and Automations
Put agents to work inside your practice and your clients' businesses.
The agents and automations that do the repeatable work: follow-ups, intake, reporting, hand-offs, and the routine tasks that eat a consultant's week. You learn which processes to automate first, how to build agents that run them reliably, and how to deploy the same systems for clients. Your practice gets lighter. Your offer gets stronger.
Track 06
Content to Client and Your AI Clones
Content that sells for you, and AI versions of you that carry it.
A content system built from your Expert Framework, so it sounds like you and speaks to exactly who you serve. Short-form video, written content, and the conversations that follow. Then your AI clones: versions of you trained on your voice and thinking, so the work keeps moving between your live hours.

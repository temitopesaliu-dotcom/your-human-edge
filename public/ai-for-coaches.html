Questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is this for?

Individual coaches already earning six or seven figures who want to learn to run their whole business with AI, not just try a few tools. If you are not at six figures yet, the readiness check still gives you a summary of what to focus on first.

Is this done-for-you implementation?

No. It is eight weeks of one-to-one coaching with me. You build your entire coaching business with AI as your execution team, so you learn to do it yourself and finish with it built.

What happens on the free call?

We talk about your business, where AI would make the biggest difference, and whether we are a fit. If we are, I walk you through what the eight weeks involve. Either way, you keep your summary of next steps.

Do I have to show my face on social media?

No. You can build an AI clone or avatar trained on your voice and thinking, which presents your content while nothing goes out in your name until you approve it.

Will AI make my coaching feel less human?

That's the risk I work against. AI takes on research, admin, content and follow-up, so you spend more time on the human work.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to know your business. The technical side is taught to you step by step, with AI as your execution team.

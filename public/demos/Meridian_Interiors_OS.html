14 · Standards

The Book

Every standard in the house documented once. Showroom presentation, client onboarding, material handling, project handover, atelier quality control. The next designer opens The Book on day one.

01
Showroom Standards
Presentation, lighting, client flow, and restaging protocol
▸
  • Every piece lit to specification within 30 minutes of showroom open. No dark corners, no unlit vignettes.
  • All price tags face down. Price is presented verbally by a consultant, never discovered by a client alone.
  • Showroom refreshed monthly. No piece sits in the same position for more than 90 days without review.
  • Coffee, water, and dates offered within 2 minutes of any client entering. Arabic hospitality is non-negotiable.
02
Client Onboarding
From first meeting to signed brief
▸
  • First meeting is always in the showroom or at the client site. Never over email. The relationship starts in person.
  • Concept board presented within 14 days of first meeting. Three directions, not one.
  • Fee structure presented with concept board. No surprises after design begins.
  • Client preferences documented: material aversions, colour sensitivities, cultural requirements, brand preferences.
03
Material Handling
Storage, QC, client approval, and wastage
▸
  • Every marble slab photographed and approved by client before cutting. No cut without sign-off.
  • Fabric stored vertically in climate-controlled room. Temperature 18-22C, humidity below 55%.
  • Timber acclimatised for 14 days in project environment before installation. No exceptions.
04
Project Handover
Snagging, walkthrough, maintenance guide
▸
  • Pre-handover snagging list completed 7 days before client walkthrough. Every item photographed.
  • Handover walkthrough conducted by lead designer + project manager. Minimum 2 hours.
  • Maintenance guide delivered in leather-bound folder. Care instructions for every material in the project.
  • Follow-up visit at 30 days and 90 days after handover. Complimentary. Non-negotiable.
05
Atelier Quality Control
Bespoke piece inspection and sign-off
▸
  • Every bespoke piece inspected by Master Artisan before leaving the workshop. Signed off on QC card.
  • Joints, finishes, and hardware checked against original specification drawing. Zero tolerance for deviation.
  • Client approval on prototype/sample before full production. No assumption of approval via silence.

01
Coach Conduct Standards
The behavioural line for how the coach shows up in the community
›
  • Never predict specific outcomes — always frame in ranges and probabilities
  • Never engage with members while emotionally activated (loss, argument, frustration)
  • Public praise, private correction — feedback always via DM, celebration always in-channel
  • Response window: within 24 hours in-community, within 2 hours for VIP direct messages
  • Do-not-disturb hours are honoured; Aria auto-queues messages during coach off-hours
02
Language & Positioning Guidelines
Words we use and words we never use — protects members and the business
›
  • "Consider", "explore", "here's how to think about" — not "you should", "buy this", "guaranteed"
  • Educational framing always: "this is how the framework applies" not "this is my recommendation for you"
  • Every reference to potential returns paired with the equivalent downside case
  • Never compare to a benchmark without disclosing the timeframe and index
  • Testimonials require signed permission and a "past performance" disclaimer
03
Alert Issuance Protocol
Exactly how a signal gets from spot to member phone
›
  • All alerts drafted in the Signal Room · never dispatched directly to members
  • 30-second review checklist: risk/reward, position sizing, ISA eligibility, jurisdiction filter
  • Auto-appended disclaimer on every dispatch — no exceptions, no manual override
  • Member acknowledgement logged for compliance record before next alert can be sent
  • Cooling-off period: 24 hours between alerts on the same ticker unless material news
04
Community Moderation Rules
What's welcome, what's not, and how borderline cases are handled
›
  • No personal recommendation requests answered in public channels — redirect to Aria or 1:1
  • Pump-and-dump, unregulated tokens, get-rich-quick — instant removal, no warning
  • Members may share their own returns; never asked to disclose account balances
  • Two-strike rule for tone violations; three-strike removal with proportional refund
  • All moderation actions logged with timestamp, reviewer, and outcome
05
Aria Prompt Library & Guardrails
What Aria says, what it never says, and how it escalates
›
  • Base prompt tuned per investor stage (Beginner / Risk-Averse / Intermediate / Pro / Trader)
  • Hard refusal list: personal recommendation, tax advice, specific product endorsement, timing calls
  • Escalation triggers: distress signals, complaint keywords, requests for personal counsel
  • Every Aria response includes an implicit "this is education, not advice" framing
  • Weekly review of Aria transcripts by coach — 20-message sample for tone drift
06
Onboarding Standards
The first 30 days of a member's experience — every touchpoint scripted
›
  • Day 0: welcome message from coach within 4 hours of join · never automated-sounding
  • Day 1: Aria intro conversation · first-lesson unlock · community group added
  • Day 7: personal check-in from coach on ISA-opening progress
  • Day 14: first assignment (open account, place first order — with hand-holding)
  • Day 30: journey review call for Growth+ tiers, chat check-in for Community
07
Escalation & Complaints Handling
When something goes wrong, exactly what happens
›
  • Any member expressing distress, loss regret, or health concern — 4-hour response SLA
  • Complaint register maintained: date, member, issue, resolution, timing
  • Refund policy: pro-rated refund within first 14 days, discretionary thereafter
  • Regulatory complaint procedure documented with FCA reporting flow if applicable
  • Post-incident review filed for every escalation over £50 loss framing
08
Regulatory Framework · Education vs Advice
The line that keeps the business legally defensible
›
  • All FinanceFlow content is educational commentary — no personal advice given, ever
  • UK members receive UK-appropriate framing; jurisdiction filter applied on every alert
  • No promotion of specific regulated products without appropriate authorisation
  • Standard disclaimer text stored centrally and version-controlled; changes are logged
  • Annual review of framework with regulatory counsel · next review scheduled
09
Data & Privacy Protocols
How member data is stored, accessed, and deleted
›
  • UK GDPR compliance · lawful basis: contract (membership) + legitimate interest (coaching)
  • Open banking data encrypted at rest, tokenised in Aria context · never sent to third parties
  • 30-day deletion SLA on any right-to-be-forgotten request
  • Aria memory layer scoped per member; not shared across accounts
  • Annual penetration test on the platform infrastructure
10
Coach Wellbeing & Boundary Rules
The business runs long-term only if the coach does
›
  • Do-not-disturb window: 20:00–07:00 local time · Aria auto-queues messages
  • One full weekend day off community per week (rotated Sat/Sun)
  • Quarterly two-week community-lead cover from a certified partner
  • Max 4 live sessions per week · beyond that, community starts to feel over-managed
  • Coach dashboard tracks hours-in-community; alert triggers at 55 hrs/wk
